#Deep Tutor v1.6.3 #DeepTutor v1.6.3 Release Notes Data de lançamento: 2026.09.02 v1.6.3 inclui um refatorador de front e back-end inovador que substitui rotas de compatibilidade e pontos de entrada de fluxo por contratos canônicos estritos, juntamente com contas de alunos e responsáveis, ações de leitura fundamentadas, conhecimento e análise mais amplos e Python 3.14. Os dados do espaço de trabalho são migrados no local, mas os marcadores e clients externos que usam URLs retirados devem ser atualizados.

Novidades

Quebrando a refatoração de front e back-end

O Home agora é executado no protocolo de giro autoritativo do servidor e em contratos de front-end digitados. Os recursos da Web possuem URLs canônicos e que podem ser marcados como favoritos; Chat, Mastery Path e Reading rejeitam segmentos extras de sessão em vez de aceitá-los silenciosamente; os caminhos de retorno de login preservam a consulta e o hash sem permitir redirecionamentos externos. Cada WebSocket está agora sob /ws/..., e as antigas entradas de compatibilidade de front-end foram removidas.

Contas de alunos com loop de guardião

Os administradores podem criar contas padrão, de aluno ou personalizadas. Os perfis dos alunos trazem idade, série, currículo, idioma, nível de leitura e preferências de explicação para o tutor; as políticas impostas pelo servidor restringem os recursos e os materiais aprovados. Os responsáveis ​​autorizados podem atribuir ou remover materiais, ler relatórios de progresso e redefinir credenciais revogáveis ​​de dispositivos com vencimento e limites diários.

A leitura se torna uma superfície de estudo ativa

Leitura imersiva adiciona citações clickable verificadas, citações e notas salvas, leitura em voz alta com base na fonte, orientação de estudo, vocabulário, questionários, tradução e captura de caderno. O Home pode transformar uma resposta em uma nota Markdown embutida, enquanto o Immersive Watching salva notas de transcrição com carimbo de data e hora e tenta novamente legendas Invidious indisponíveis.

O trabalho de longa duração agora é interrompido e retomado de forma limpa

A geração de livros tem uma pausa persistente que cancela trabalhos em fila e em andamento sem perder páginas concluídas, é retomada somente mediante solicitação e pausa automaticamente falhas repetidas de modelo. Os fluxos de bate-papo, livro, conhecimento e memória usam cursores monotônicos, reprodução, desduplicação e recuperação de estado terminal; o julgamento do questionário e as consultas aos subagentes podem ser interrompidas e limitadas por tempos limite.

Mais mecanismos de conhecimento, recuperação local mais rápida, análise mais ampla

Conecte uma base de conhecimento WeKnora auto-hospedada sem copiar seus documentos ou criar um índice local. LlamaIndex ganha reclassificação de codificador cruzado opcional e índices exatos planos ou HNSW FAISS; LightRAG pode usar um modelo dedicado. A análise de documentos agora segue o catálogo de formatos atuais de cada mecanismo, incluindo ampla cobertura de Docling, Tika, PyMuPDF4LLM, LiteParse e markitdown.

Mais opções de provedor e autoria

DashScope une geração de texto para fala, fala para texto, imagem e vídeo. Perfis compatíveis com OpenAI podem selecionar explicitamente respostas ou conclusões de bate-papo, com estado de raciocínio preservado entre turnos. O Co-Writer importa rascunhos .docx e exporta Word, e Configurações adiciona ferramentas pesquisáveis.

Relatórios, sandboxes e inicializadores mais seguros

A Deep Research tenta novamente seções incompletas do relatório sem vazar prosa parcial duplicada. A execução local impõe limites de saída e de processo. O iniciador adiciona um comando de inicialização do macOS, comportamento do Windows seguro para navegador, operação desanexada/sem navegador e deeptutor stop; fluxos de bate-papo interrompidos também se recuperam de maneira mais confiável.

  • Atualize marcadores e interfaces personalizadas. As rotas de sessão canônicas são /chat/{sessionId}, /mastery/{pathId}/sessions/{sessionId} e /reading/{workspaceId}/sessions/{sessionId}. DeepTutor v1.6.3

Highlights

  • Breaking route and WebSocket contracts — Update bookmarks and custom clients before upgrading: legacy /space/learning/*, /partners/groups, /settings/about, and API-prefixed sockets no longer work. Sessions now use canonical URLs such as /chat/{sessionId}, resource links such as /notebooks/{notebookId}, and WebSockets exclusively under /ws/...; extra session segments are rejected rather than silently ignored.

  • Durable pause, resume, and stream recovery — Book-generation pauses now cancel queued and in-flight work while preserving completed pages, resume only when requested, and automatically pause after repeated model failures. Stream consumers should use the new monotonic cursors, replay, deduplication, and terminal states to avoid duplicate rendering, while quiz judging and subagent consultations gain cancellation and timeouts.

  • Server-enforced learner and guardian controls — Learner profiles carry age, curriculum, reading level, and explanation preferences into tutoring, but restrictions and approved materials are enforced server-side rather than relying on frontend configuration. Guardians can manage materials and progress and reset expiring, rate-limited device credentials—useful for shared-device deployments, but requiring explicit role and credential setup.

  • Reading becomes a verifiable study workspace — Immersive Reading now combines clickable verified citations, saved notes, source-grounded read-aloud, vocabulary, quizzes, translation, and notebook capture. Watching adds timestamped transcript notes and retries unavailable Invidious captions, making study artifacts traceable to their sources instead of detached AI summaries.

  • Federated knowledge and retrieval options — Self-hosted WeKnora can be queried without copying documents or rebuilding a local index. LlamaIndex adds cross-encoder reranking and exact-flat or HNSW FAISS indexes, LightRAG supports a dedicated model, and parser integrations now enforce current format catalogs—refresh optional extras to the new minimums, including Docling 2.123.1 and PyMuPDF4LLM 1.28.2.

  • Python 3.14 and provider compatibility — The core app and CLI support Python 3.14, but GraphRAG remains limited to 3.11–3.13, and LlamaIndex falls back to vector retrieval there when optional BM25 dependencies are unavailable. OpenAI-compatible profiles must explicitly choose Responses or Chat Completions; select the intended API to preserve reasoning state consistently across turns.

DeepTutor v1.6.3 Release Notes

Release Date: 2026.09.02

v1.6.3 includes a breaking front- and back-end refactor that replaces compatibility routes and stream entry points with strict canonical contracts, alongside learner and guardian accounts, grounded Reading actions, broader knowledge and parsing, and Python 3.14. Workspace data migrates in place, but bookmarks and external clients using retired URLs must update.

What's New

Breaking front- and back-end refactor

Home now runs on the server-authoritative turn protocol and typed frontend contracts. Web resources have canonical, bookmarkable URLs; Chat, Mastery Path, and Reading reject extra session segments instead of silently accepting them; login return paths preserve query and hash without permitting external redirects. Every WebSocket is now under /ws/..., and the old frontend compatibility entrances have been removed.

Learner accounts with a guardian loop

Admins can create Standard, Learner, or Custom accounts. Learner profiles carry age, grade, curriculum, language, reading level, and explanation preferences into the tutor; server-enforced policies constrain capabilities and approved materials. Authorized guardians can assign or remove materials, read progress reports, and reset revocable device credentials with expiry and daily limits.

Reading becomes an active study surface

Immersive Reading adds verified clickable citations, saved citations and notes, source-grounded read-aloud, study guidance, vocabulary, quizzes, translation, and notebook capture. Home can turn a reply into an inline Markdown note, while Immersive Watching saves timestamped transcript notes and retries unavailable Invidious captions.

Long-running work now stops and resumes cleanly

Book generation has a persistent pause that cancels queued and in-flight work without losing completed pages, resumes only on request, and automatically pauses repeated model failures. Chat, Book, Knowledge, and Memory streams use monotonic cursors, replay, deduplication, and terminal-state recovery; quiz judging and subagent consultations can be stopped and are bounded by timeouts.

More knowledge engines, faster local retrieval, broader parsing

Connect a self-hosted WeKnora knowledge base without copying its documents or building a local index. LlamaIndex gains optional cross-encoder reranking and exact-flat or HNSW FAISS indexes; LightRAG can use a dedicated model. Document parsing now follows each engine's current format catalog, including broad Docling, Tika, PyMuPDF4LLM, LiteParse, and markitdown coverage.

More provider and authoring choices

DashScope joins text-to-speech, speech-to-text, image, and video generation. OpenAI-compatible profiles can explicitly select Responses or Chat Completions, with reasoning state preserved across turns. Co-Writer imports .docx drafts and exports Word, and Settings adds searchable tools.

Safer reports, sandboxes, and launchers

Deep Research retries incomplete report sections without leaking duplicate partial prose. Local execution enforces output and process limits. The launcher adds a macOS startup command, browser-safe Windows behavior, detached/no-browser operation, and deeptutor stop; interrupted chat streams also recover more reliably.

Upgrade Notes

Run pip install -U deeptutor; Docker users pull ghcr.io/hkuds/deeptutor:latest. Existing settings, accounts, knowledge indexes, books, Reading state, and sessions need no manual migration or re-index; startup migrates legacy chat and workspace-preference data in place.

  • Update bookmarks and custom frontends. Canonical session routes are /chat/{sessionId}, /mastery/{pathId}/sessions/{sessionId}, and /reading/{workspaceId}/sessions/{sessionId}. Resource routes are /books/{bookId}/pages/{pageId}, /notebooks/{notebookId}, and /knowledge-bases/{kbName}. Retired /space/learning/*, /partners/groups, and /settings/about entrances no longer redirect; use /mastery, the Partner workspace, and /settings#about.

  • Update WebSocket clients. The canonical namespace is /ws, /ws/books, /ws/questions/*, /ws/knowledge-bases/{kbName}/progress, /ws/mastery-paths, /ws/partners/{partnerId}, and /ws/partner-groups/{groupId}. Legacy API-prefixed sockets are removed.

  • Python 3.14 is supported by the core app and CLI. GraphRAG remains on Python 3.11–3.13. On 3.14, LlamaIndex falls back to vector retrieval when the optional BM25 dependency is unavailable.

  • Refresh optional parser extras. The supported floors are markitdown 0.1.7, Docling 2.123.1, PyMuPDF4LLM 1.28.2, LiteParse 2.14.2, MinerU 3.4.5, and Apache Tika 4. Older engines report that they need an update instead of running against an incompatible API.

  • The English and Chinese guides have been updated together at deeptutor.info.
