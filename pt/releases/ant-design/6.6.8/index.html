ant-design 6.6.8

Highlights

  • Reliable confirmation flows — Modal and Popconfirm custom button onClick handlers no longer suppress confirmation or cancellation callbacks; audit custom actions if they depended on the broken behavior (#59294).

  • Theme-consistent modal content — Confirmation dialog text now respects colorText, fontSize, and lineHeight; custom themes should be visually rechecked after upgrading (#59627).

  • More stable interactions and event handling — Pagination now prevents accidental text selection after repeated jump-button clicks, while Message no longer intercepts pointer events outside its content (#59638, #59622).

  • Accessibility fixes — Pagination restores visible keyboard-focus indicators, and Progress reports bounded overall aria-valuenow values instead of success.percent, correcting screen-reader announcements (#59573, #59634).

  • Improved Upload failure handling — Failed image decoding now resolves or cleans up preview tasks instead of leaving them pending and leaking memory, making repeated invalid uploads safer in long-running sessions (#59583).

  • TypeScript and visual consistency — List.dataSource accepts readonly arrays, while Card title spacing and List virtual-scrollbar theming are corrected (#59642, #59613, #59643).

  • No breaking changes.
