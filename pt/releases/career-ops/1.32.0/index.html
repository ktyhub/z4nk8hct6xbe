career-ops career-ops-v1.32.0

Highlights

  • Expanded sourcing and evidence-based triage — The Built In aggregator plus Feishu Jobs, MokaHR, and Garena providers broaden discovery without custom scanners (#3645, #3491, #3567). Portal sweeps now validate job_boards, not only tracked_companies (#3448). Oferta Block B adds evidence-tiered requirement importance with stable decisive columns (#3596, #3660), while h1b-sponsor surfaces DOL sponsorship history for US roles (#2635)—useful historical evidence, not a guarantee of current sponsorship.
  • Auditable scans with less repeated work — Durable machine-readable scan receipts (#3532) make results suitable for downstream automation and auditing. Permanently dead ATS boards are remembered (#2869), and Workday requisitions duplicated across a tenant’s sites are collapsed (#3446), reducing wasted requests and duplicate leads. Under --json, progress now goes to stderr, keeping stdout machine-parseable.
  • Stricter, more consistent CV generation — Builders reject section keys they never consume (#3524), block delegated authorship escalation (#3657), and run the fact gate before rendering (#3647). Canonical PDF tailoring order is accepted (#3642), and emailed fit points now align with the tailored PDF through the pdf-index row (#3473). Migration: remove unsupported payload keys and regenerate affected PDFs; previously accepted malformed payloads will now fail fast.
  • CJK support across rendering and parsing — The LaTeX/Overleaf path gains CJK rendering through Tectonic (#3554). Fallback fonts remain active for Japanese, Simplified Chinese, and Korean (#3531), and Traditional Chinese profiles honor font_family (#3525). Skill-gap extraction also recognizes requirement headings in both Traditional and Simplified Chinese (#3631).
  • Analytics and automation behavior changed — Applied no longer counts as a positive outcome (#3667), and funnel calibration incorporates status-log history (#3494), so reported conversion rates may shift. Entering Applied through set-status now seeds the follow-up immediately (#3470). Locked writers protect concurrent updates (#3462, #3479), malformed application IDs fail instead of being coerced (#3485), and updater installation requires confirmation (#2866)—review validation and unattended scripts accordingly.
  • Breaking changes — None declared in the changelog.
