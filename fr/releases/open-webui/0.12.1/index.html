open-webui v0.12.1

Added

  • 🧱 External apps in channels. Administrators can now turn on Apps in Admin Settings > General, or with "ENABLE_APPS", and connect external apps in the new Apps tab of Admin Settings by their HTTPS address, reviewing the permissions each app asks for, such as reading its mentions, reading channel messages or posting messages, and choosing who may use it; channel managers then add an app to a channel with Add Members in the channel info, where it is listed with the members and its own profile picture, and mentioning it with @ lets it answer in the channel with replies that can be shown only to the person who asked, show cards with a title, fields and a thumbnail, offer buttons or links, follow up with a later message for longer tasks or provide a Markdown file to download. Apps on a private network address can be reached over plain HTTP once their address is listed in "APP_ALLOWED_PRIVATE_ORIGINS".
