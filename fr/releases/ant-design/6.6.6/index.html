ant-design 6.6.6

Highlights

  • Table correctness — Tree-table “Select all data” now includes descendants, and preserved keys no longer make rowSelection.onSelect return undefined; custom filter popupRender, ConfigProvider-only scroll resets, and fixed-column selection layering are also restored, reducing silent selection and navigation errors (#59342, #59449, #59393, #59394, #58609).

  • Bulk-selection semantics — Transfer and Tree.DirectoryTree Shift-selection now respect search results and node eligibility: filtered-out items are excluded, ranges stay correct after searches change, numeric tree keys work with defaultExpandAll, and disabled or non-selectable nodes are skipped. Controlled Transfer removal also clears stale checked state, and selectAllLabels={0} is honored (#59348, #59375, #59341, #59428, #59487).

  • Provider and locale behavior — Range pickers now apply ConfigProvider.locale.DatePicker, AutoComplete no longer inherits select.allowClear, and TreeSelect/Mentions pass their own component names to renderEmpty; missing DatePicker placeholders and Carousel arrow labels are also filled in across remaining locales (#59356, #59358, #59364, #59351).

  • Interaction regressions — OTP paste focuses the correct cell, Typography remains editable with onClick, Upload file-link handlers fire, closable.disabled blocks Alert dismissal, Tour callbacks receive mouse events, and Notification timers resume after a hovered notice closes. Numeric 0 also renders instead of disappearing in List, Divider, and Drawer (#59479, #59325, #59324, #59432, #59332, #59435, #59118, #59517, #59437).

  • TypeScript contracts — Declarations now exclude unsupported verticalSwiping and Form.Item/Rate/QRCode props, so audit for no-op props that may now fail type-checking; Upload custom requests also expose abort in their types, enabling typed cancellation (#59366, #59373, #59379, #59408, #59382).

  • Breaking changes — No runtime breaking changes; TypeScript users may see new errors for unsupported props.
