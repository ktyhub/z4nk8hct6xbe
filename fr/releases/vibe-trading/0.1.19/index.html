Vibe-Trading v0.1.19

Cumule 299 commits / 116 demandes d'extraction fusionnées depuis 0.1.18, plus 21 demandes d'extraction dont le travail a abouti via les commits du responsable, sur 26 contributeurs. Le titre est une recherche qui survit au chat qui l'a produite. Un fini l'analyse peut être enregistrée en tant qu'artefact de recherche versionné avec ses preuves références et données à la date du, recherchées ultérieurement et révisées au lieu de écrasé ; un tour de suivi ou une session ultérieure trouve déjà les backtests parcourir list_backtest_runs et lire leurs artefacts au lieu de les exécuter eux encore; et l'objectif d'un objectif de recherche peut être modifié sans perdre son des preuves. Le trading sur papier ajoute une simulation de cinq minutes d'actions A opt-in qui règle une session terminée barre par barre sur un nouveau cycle de vie de commande, avec Règles de remplissage de limite de JoinQuant ; les comptes enregistrés sont toujours réglés quotidiennement. Gildata requêtes financières, sélection des actions de la période de rapport et portefeuille Shannon le diagnostic arrive sous forme d'opt-ins distincts pour les titulaires de compte Gildata, Gildata les guillemets ne sont utilisés que lorsqu'ils sont nommés et l'intégration QVeris est supprimée. L'Argentine (actions BYMA et CEDEAR) rejoint en tant que marché de backtest, web_search peut utiliser la recherche hébergée de Parallel, et le Codex OAuth répertorie les modèles offres de compte connecté. Les deux régressions 0.1.18 sont corrigées : une nouvelle tentative l'erreur du fournisseur n'interrompt plus les décisions en direct pour la journée, et une recherche la réponse de l'objectif qui dépasse son budget symbolique est fournie. La majeure partie du reste est exactitude : corrections de formule pour les alphas GTJA et Qlib, contrat à terme spécifications et frais, formulaires de pagination et de ticker du chargeur, mise à la terre de documenter les figures et leurs unités, les tâches d'arrière-plan qui appartiennent à la session qui les a démarrés et survivent à un redémarrage, et des canaux de discussion qui ne sont plus appelez un transcripteur vocal qui n'existe pas. L'interface utilisateur Web reçoit un nouvel accueil écran et une palette de papier chaud en clair et sombre. Mise à niveau : une clé QVeris stockée n'est plus lue, et un GILDATA_TOKEN seul n'active plus aucune fonctionnalité Gildata. Paires et scalaires (lire >=, comme le live le scanner les lit) mappent désormais sur des limites inclusives, chaque côté s'applique sur son propre, et le scanner et le moteur partagent un même vocabulaire d'opérateur. Un > strict ou <, ==, != ou un marché sans chaîne, que le moteur ne peut pas exprimer exactement, génère désormais une erreur au lieu d'être élargi.

Vibe-Trading v0.1.19

Highlights

  • Research now persists across sessions — Versioned research artifacts (#2079, #2159) preserve evidence references, provenance and data-as-of dates as immutable revisions; updating via artifact_id keeps subject and universe fixed, while changed content defaults to unverified rather than appearing verified or freshly researched. list_backtest_runs (#2074) lets later turns reuse prior runs instead of repeating expensive backtests, with grounding gated on matching run-card hashes and metrics. Goal objectives can also be edited without losing criteria or evidence, using expected_goal_id to prevent stale writes (#2093).

  • Five-minute A-share simulation is opt-in — paper_trade_step with interval="5m" settles completed CN-A session bars using Tushare stk_mins, one symbol per step (#2110, #2111). JoinQuant-style limit matching requires a strict breach of the limit price, fills at the limit or a better reference price, cancels unfilled day orders, and excludes auctions and zero-volume bars (#2127). Results use pt-v1.2 and pt-rules-v2 fingerprints; calls without the new fields remain byte-identical to pt-v1.1, and saved paper accounts remain daily-only. Partial fills and volume participation are still unimplemented.

  • Data-source configuration requires migration — QVeris integration is removed and stored QVeris keys are no longer read. GILDATA_TOKEN alone no longer enables Gildata features: adopters must explicitly select fin_query, symbol_search, stock_selection and/or shannon in Settings → Data Sources or through VIBE_TRADING_GILDATA_FEATURES. These provide normalized financial queries, report-period screening and portfolio diagnosis, with Shannon also requiring account entitlement. Without opt-in, Gildata remains inactive even with a valid token.

  • Argentina’s BYMA market is supported for backtests — .BA symbols now select a long-only, cash-only ARS engine automatically; do not set engine (#2071). Leverage other than 1, short selling and mixing .BA with other markets in a composite pool are refused. Settlement follows observed-session T+2 rules, or T+1 from 2024-05-27. CEDEAR ratio changes and tick rules must be declared: ambiguous or undeclared price-step events fail the run rather than silently misprice positions. Brokerage, exchange fees, fixed fees and slippage are simulation inputs, not official tariffs.

  • Factor and alpha tooling exposes leakage risk — The new combination module supports equal, IC, ICIR, explicit and time-varying weights; IC/ICIR use only earlier observations and preserve factor sign (#1953, #1959, #1963, #2046). Missing factors are dropped and remaining weights renormalized, but the module does not size positions, apply costs or neutralize exposures. alpha_bench now reports per-field data_availability metadata, including coverage and verified/assumed/unknown/failed status (#2057); SEC fundamentals remain explicitly non-PIT-safe because filings lack publication timestamps.

  • Connectivity and decision reliability improve — Codex OAuth discovers the signed-in account’s available models, reasoning efforts and defaults, falling back to manual configuration if discovery fails (#2080). Local IBKR and Futu connections can now retain independent host/port endpoints for checks, portfolios and quotes (#1983). Optional Parallel-hosted search slots into web_search behind VIBE_TRADING_SEARCH_PARALLEL=1, falling through to existing providers on failure (#2113). Both 0.1.18 regressions are fixed: retried provider errors no longer halt live decisions for the day, and goal answers that cross their token budget are delivered.

Rolls up 299 commits / 116 merged pull requests since 0.1.18, plus 21 pull requests whose work landed through maintainer commits, from 26 contributors.

The headline is research that outlives the chat that produced it. A finished analysis can be saved as a versioned research artifact with its evidence references and data-as-of date, searched later, and revised instead of overwritten; a follow-up turn or a later session finds the backtests already run through list_backtest_runs and reads their artifacts instead of running them again; and a research goal's objective can be edited without losing its evidence. Paper trading adds an opt-in A-share five-minute simulation that settles a completed session bar by bar on a new order lifecycle, with JoinQuant's limit-fill rules; saved accounts still settle daily. Gildata financial queries, report-period stock screening and Shannon portfolio diagnosis arrive as separate opt-ins for Gildata account holders, Gildata quotes are used only when named, and the QVeris integration is removed. Argentina (BYMA shares and CEDEARs) joins as a backtest market, web_search can use Parallel's hosted search, and Codex OAuth lists the models the signed-in account offers. Both 0.1.18 regressions are fixed: a retried provider error no longer halts live decisions for the day, and a research goal's answer that crosses its token budget is delivered. Most of the rest is correctness: formula corrections to GTJA and Qlib alphas, futures contract specifications and fees, loader paging and ticker forms, grounding of document figures and their units, background tasks that belong to the session that started them and survive a restart, and chat channels that no longer call a voice transcriber that does not exist. The Web UI gets a new welcome screen and a warm paper palette in light and dark.

Upgrading: a stored QVeris key is no longer read, and a GILDATA_TOKEN alone no longer enables any Gildata feature. Pick the features in Settings → Data Sources or VIBE_TRADING_GILDATA_FEATURES.

Added

  • Versioned research artifacts (#2079, #2159, thanks @he-yufeng; closes #1909, #2158). A finished analysis can be saved as an immutable revision with its subject, universe, data-as-of date, evidence references, verification status and optional hypothesis, goal, session or run links, through the save_research_artifact / search_research_artifacts / get_research_artifact agent tools, vibe-trading research-artifact save|search|show|revision, /api/research-artifacts and a Web page in all eleven languages. Passing an artifact_id appends a linked revision; the subject and universe cannot change. Repeating identical content and provenance returns the existing revision, and changed content or evidence defaults to unverified: saving is not verification and does not make old data fresh. The index lives in the runtime sessions.db unless VIBE_TRADING_RESEARCH_ARTIFACT_DB_PATH names another file; the idempotency lookup is indexed (#2159). The REST save route accepts at most 1,000 evidence objects (1,000,000 characters serialized) and ids of at most 200 characters.

  • list_backtest_runs finds the backtests earlier turns and sessions already ran (#2074, thanks @zeus229), so a follow-up reads their artifacts with read_run_artifact instead of re-running them. It lists runs from the req.json and run_card.json the runtime already writes, newest first and paged, with each backtest's absolute run_dir; the agent tool defaults to the current session, the MCP tool to every run. Assistant replies in the history carry [prev_run: <id>]. The grounding gate adopts a prior run read this way only when its run card and metrics.csv agree and every table it registers matches the run card manifest hash. The session filter is not an access control (runs have no owner), and a run the model forged in an earlier turn is not detected.

  • A research goal's objective can be edited in place (#2093, thanks @Shizoqua). update_research_goal (agent tool and session-isolated MCP tool) replaces the objective or display summary while keeping the same goal id, criteria and evidence, with an optional expected_goal_id stale-write guard. Replacing the goal entirely is still start_research_goal.

  • Codex OAuth model discovery (#2080, thanks @zeus229). Web Settings lists the models the signed-in ChatGPT account returns (entries marked visibility: list, including OAuth-only ones) with their display names, supported reasoning efforts and default effort, retrying once with a refreshed token on a 401. A failed catalog request keeps the configured or default model and manual entry available.

  • Opt-in five-minute A-share paper steps (#2110, #2111, #2066, thanks @shadowinlife and @Jackzigen). paper_trade_step (agent and MCP), POST /api/paper-trade/step and vibe-trading paper step accept interval="5m", plus optional session (auto / cn_a), path_model (bar_median / bar_close) and fill_model (all_or_none only). One step settles one completed cn_a session bar by bar over its 48 continuous five-minute bars from Tushare stk_mins: a market order fills at the first bar whose checks pass, zero-volume bars never fill, an order blocked by the price limit waits for a later bar and is rejected if still blocked at the close, and unfilled day orders are cancelled at session end and listed in canceled_orders. The 09:30 auction print is not matched; halts and auction-window bars are reported in events. The measured provider budget allows one symbol per step, only ordinary Shanghai/Shenzhen stocks have a five-minute leg (no ETFs, indices or Beijing listings), and strategy mode needs an explicit UNIVERSE. Such a call returns schema_version pt-v1.2 with an engine_fingerprint; a call without the new fields is byte-identical to pt-v1.1. Saved paper accounts (CLI paper advance, the REST advance route and the Web page) stay daily-only. Matching runs on a new bar-agnostic order book (#2066) with replay-stable order ids and day-order cancellation. Partial fills and volume participation remain open under #1938.

  • Five-minute limit orders follow JoinQuant's bar matching (#2127, thanks @shadowinlife). A resting buy limit fills only when a bar's low trades strictly below the limit, a sell only when its high trades strictly above it, and the fill is at the limit price; a bar that only touches the limit leaves the order resting. On the first traded bar, a reference price already better than the limit fills at that price instead. Price-limit and cash checks use the price the fill would execute at. The matching rules are versioned pt-rules-v2, so a v1.2 result's engine_fingerprint differs from one computed under pt-rules-v1 with the same bars and settings.

  • A gateway address per local IBKR or Futu connection (#1983, thanks @TerryF7). A local IBKR (TWS/Gateway) or Futu OpenD connection can store its own host and port: through Host / Port on its card in the connection center (saved, then tested), PUT /api/connections/{id}/endpoint, or vibe-trading connector setup ... --host H --port P (both required). The address is used for that connection's checks, portfolio reads and quotes and by agent read tools given its connection_id, and the connection list shows the effective endpoint. Only a hostname or IP is accepted (no scheme, path or credentials), and other transports refuse an endpoint. Existing broker configuration remains the fallback; order placement and paper/live account checks are unchanged.

  • BYMA backtests for Argentine shares and CEDEARs (#2071, thanks @zeus229). .BA codes used to resolve to their market and then fail closed in the runner; they now select a long-only, cash-only ARS engine on their own (do not set engine; leverage other than 1 and allow_short are refused, and .BA cannot share a composite pool with another market). Normal settlement is T+2, T+1 from 2024-05-27, counted in observed sessions (dates on which some loaded instrument traded); zero-volume holiday bars are dropped and never fill, and a settlement past the last bar is reported as beyond_loaded_data. Sale proceeds fund only purchases settling on the same or a later session; acquired shares may be resold before they settle. CEDEARs must be declared in ar_instruments with a ratio_history: each declared ratio change is checked against the observed price step and earlier bars are restated, an ambiguous step or an undeclared one-bar move (40% CEDEAR, 75% share) fails the run, and a position held through the change can be sold only once its earliest settlement reaches the declared credit_date. A tick rule must be declared (ar_tick_table from the BYMA Circular 3569 annex versions, or a per-instrument tick_table / tick_size); fills round against the trader. Lots default to one share and ar_brokerage_rate / ar_exchange_fee_rate / ar_fixed_fee / ar_slippage are simulation inputs, not official tariffs; all of it is labelled in the ar_data_basis metric, artifacts/byma_preparation.json and artifacts/cash_ledger.csv.

  • Optional Parallel search in web_search (#2113, thanks @RowanAldean). With VIBE_TRADING_SEARCH_PARALLEL=1, web_search queries Parallel's anonymous hosted MCP endpoint (free and rate-limited; no account, key or new dependency) after a configured Aliyun IQS and before the ddgs engines and CN fallbacks. Off by default. A Parallel failure falls through to the existing sources and its reason is prefixed to any final error; results are security-scanned and snippets cut to 300 characters.

  • Gildata combined financial queries and search candidates (#2117, thanks @TWJxi and @lishuanghong1989-netizen). The gildata_fin_query agent tool sends one natural-language question (up to 4,000 characters) covering a security's market, financial and operating indicators to Gildata FinQuery. Results keep actual periods, disclosure/trading dates and quote adjustment; amounts are converted to base currency units and percents kept as points, each with a ready figure declaration for the grounding gate. The symbol_search feature adds Gildata's A-share, HK and US candidates to search_symbol, exact matches first. Needs GILDATA_TOKEN (a commercial Gildata account) plus the fin_query / symbol_search opt-in; a FinQuery failure returns an error without trying another source.

  • Gildata stock screening with report-period indicators (#2118, thanks @TWJxi and @lishuanghong1989-netizen). gildata_stock_selection sends one question (universe, ranking or screen and its date, plus the indicators and report periods wanted, e.g. the ten heaviest CSI 300 weights with their 2023 and 2024 revenue) to Gildata's SmartStockSelection and returns the tables with the same unit normalization as gildata_fin_query. topk is a positive integer, or -1 when all results are explicitly wanted. Read-only; needs GILDATA_TOKEN and the stock_selection opt-in.

  • Shannon portfolio style and risk diagnosis (#2119, thanks @TWJxi and @lishuanghong1989-netizen). With the shannon opt-in and a token whose Gildata account carries the service, the Web Correlation page gains a Shannon tab (/api/shannon/status|catalog|diagnosis): paste up to 2,000 A-share stocks with all weights summing to 100%, or none for equal weight, and see factor exposures, per-stock characteristics and each holding's share of portfolio volatility. Indices, duplicates and partial weights are refused; the date defaults to the latest published analysis date, and a result dated after the requested date is refused. A release follow-up moved the tab's text into all eleven UI languages.

  • Factor panels combine into one composite score with walk-forward weights (#1953, thanks @BHhhh-123; #1959, #1963, thanks @rudycelekli; #2046, thanks @dajiaohuang). A new Quant Library module, combination (combine_factors, also through quantlib_call), merges several time × asset factor panels with equal, ic or icir weights, or an explicit mapping or time × factor table for any other method. IC and ICIR weights at a row use only IC observations from earlier rows and keep their sign, so a reversed factor gets a negative weight. Factors are z-scored per row by default; a factor missing on a row is left out and the others renormalised, and a row with no factor or no weight yet stays NaN. Diagnostics report per-factor and composite IC/IR, mean factor rank correlation, weight turnover and rows without weights; the IC figures are full-sample diagnostics, not the weights that were applied. Fixed before release: the insufficient="equal" fallback weights 1/n instead of 1 per factor, a single IC observation reports no ic_std instead of 0.0, and explicit weights near the float limit normalise without overflowing. It does not size positions, apply costs or neutralise exposures.

  • Alpha bench results say when each input could have been known (#2057, thanks @BHhhh-123). Bench results, plain and --strict, including those run through the alpha_bench agent tool, MCP and alpha compare, add meta.data_availability (alpha-bench.data-availability/v1): market adapter, venue, currency, timezone, calendar and observed window, and per field its source, availability mode, non-null coverage and a status of verified, assumed, unknown or failed. A declared alpha input missing from the panel is failed. SEC fundamentals stay unknown and not PIT-safe, because a filing carries a date but no publication time, even with the bench's one-bar lag. Built-in adapters cover csi300, sp500 and btc-usdt; other universes get a generic unknown context. The contract records; no IC changes.

Bench results add meta.research (factor-research.run/v1): the declared mode (explore, strict, locked), the lifecycle stage (factor_screening through monitoring), universe, period, factor ids, lineage, transformations and evaluation settings (horizon, forward-return sampling, method, and the strict runner's out-of-sample boundary). alpha bench --research-mode / --research-stage, the alpha_bench agent tool and MCP set them and refuse an unknown value; REST and alpha compare results carry the defaults. The merge exposed the mode on those surfaces and defaults it to strict for --strict runs and explore otherwise. The values are declarations: locked certifies no locked test set, and no IC, weight or backtest changes.

  • The documentation wiki can be crawled and indexed (#2165, thanks @hnq-ai). vibetrading.wiki now ships a real robots.txt (AI crawlers allowed, /api/ disallowed, a Sitemap: line) and a 42-URL sitemap.xml, both served with their own content types, plus a bilingual 404 page. Regressions in 0.1.18

  • A retried provider error no longer halts live model decisions for the day. 0.1.18 announced every failed stream, compaction call or cancelled response that reported no usage as missing usage, which the live decision budget treats as a reason to halt; a connection reset followed by a successful retry stopped live decisions until the next UTC day. The grounding gate

  • Figures quoted from an uploaded document can be grounded (#2058, thanks @Jackzigen; closes #2050). read_document answers in prose, so its numbers never became evidence and the gate cut every figure quoted from a PDF. A document the run wrote, or one inside the run directory, is not evidence.   • Percentage-point figures ground against the bare declaration (#2108, thanks @Jackzigen; closes #2107). 1.13个百分点 is conventionally declared 1.13; the gate cut such figures even when the document held the value.   • Historical VaR and CVaR use the documented number of tail losses (#1950, thanks @rudycelekli). The report's RANK(VWAP − MAX(VWAP,15)) ^ DELTA(CLOSE,5) was computed as |base| ^ |exponent|, so after a five-day fall a base of 0.5 and a change of −2 gave 0.25 instead of 4. The base is a percentile rank in (0, 1], so the power is now taken directly with the signed change. The merge also corrected the golden fixture and the factor's English and Chinese notes. Values change wherever the five-day close change is negative (and, via #2039, wherever the VWAP scale mattered). A release follow-up keeps the value missing whenever the base or the exponent is: IEEE pow gives 1 for NaN ** 0 and 1 ** NaN, so a flat close during the 15-bar VWAP warmup, or the top-ranked name on a bar with no close change, had started to report 1.0.

  • GTJA alpha 55 has the swing index's third denominator branch (#2036, thanks @rudycelekli). The denominator chose between the high-gap form |H−C₋₁| + |L−C₋₁|/2 + |C₋₁−O₋₁|/4 and the low-gap form only. When neither of those gaps is the largest range, the report uses |H−L₋₁| + |C₋₁−O₋₁|/4, and the code now does too. Values change on those bars and on every 20-day sum that contains one.

  • GTJA alpha 170 scales by the 5-day mean of HIGH (#2037, thanks @rudycelekli). The middle term HIGH × RANK(HIGH − CLOSE) / (SUM(HIGH,5)/5) divided by the 5-day mean of CLOSE instead of HIGH, contrary to the report and the factor's own formula metadata. Every value changes.

  • GTJA alpha 140 drops an extra TSRANK (#2038, thanks @rudycelekli). The correlation leg ranked the correlation over 7 days before decaying it. The report's leg is TSRANK(DECAYLINEAR(CORR(TSRANK(CLOSE,8), TSRANK(MEAN(VOLUME,60),20), 8), 7), 3), which is what is computed now. Values change wherever that leg is the smaller of the two.

  • GTJA alpha 101 uses the report's right-hand leg (#2040, thanks @rudycelekli). It compared against RANK(CORR(RANK(HIGH), RANK(MEAN(VOLUME,10)), 11)); the report's leg is RANK(CORR(RANK(HIGH × 0.1 + VWAP × 0.9), RANK(VOLUME), 11)). Code, formula metadata and golden fixture now follow the report, and a bar is NaN unless VWAP was observed across the 11-day window. The 0 / −1 signal flips on bars where the comparison changes.

  • Qlib BETA and RESI are least-squares trend statistics (#2062, thanks @he-yufeng). Alpha158 defines BETAd = Slope(close, d) / close and RESId = Resi(close, d) / close. The five BETA factors (5, 10, 20, 30, 60) computed the endpoint slope (close − close₋d) / (d × close), and the five RESI factors the gap to the moving average (close − mean(close, d)) / close. Both now use new ts_slope / ts_resi operators: the OLS slope of close on time over a full window, and the latest close's residual from that fit. Every value changes.

  • Qlib ROC and CORD match Alpha158 (#2069, thanks @he-yufeng). The five ROC factors returned the d-day return close / close₋d − 1; Alpha158's ROCd is close₋d / close, a decreasing function of that return, so each ROC factor's rank IC changes sign and a bench may now call one reversed that it called alive, or the reverse. The five CORD factors correlated the price ratio with log((v + 1) / (v₋₁ + 1)) instead of Alpha158's log(v / v₋₁ + 1), which they now use. Formula metadata follows.

Valuation, rigor checks and report audit

  • Valuation workbooks keep text literal (#1949, thanks @rudycelekli). export_dcf_workbook, export_comps_workbook and export_three_statement_workbook let openpyxl read a string starting with = as a formula and #N/A as an Excel error, so an assumption basis such as = 2% inflation + 1% real growth read back empty. Every text cell is now written as a string; numbers and booleans keep their types.

  • financial_rigor refuses non-finite and boolean inputs (#2053, thanks @yufengsika). NaN, Infinity and true (read as 1) were accepted and answered status: "ok" with NaN or Infinity in a payload that is not valid JSON, and a finite price such as 1e308 could overflow to infinity. Every command that converts a number now returns an error envelope for such input, and a result that cannot be written as finite JSON is an error.

  • Earnings and FCF yield are omitted at a zero price (#2033, thanks @rudycelekli). verify_valuation raised DivisionByZero when the price was 0 and EPS or FCF per share was not. Those two yields are now left out, as dividend yield already was; P/E, P/FCF and the other ratios with price in the numerator are still reported.

  • Report audit skips fenced examples (#1951, thanks @rudycelekli). report_audit dropped fenced code only from its label: value pass, so a template table inside a fence was sampled as a real claim, and tilde fences or a longer fence around a shorter one were mishandled. Fenced blocks (backtick or tilde, closed by a fence at least as long, or unclosed to the end) are now blanked before both passes, keeping the report's line numbers.

Governance ledger and entity ingestion

  • The audit ledger rotates past its first segment (#1962, thanks @rudycelekli). rotate_if_needed verified only the active file before sealing it. After one rotation that file legitimately starts mid-chain, so the check reported a sequence gap on a healthy ledger and every later rotation raised LedgerCorruptionError. It now verifies the whole history, sealed segments included. A maintainer follow-up numbers a new archive after the highest existing one (past gaps and beyond four digits), orders segments numerically, and seals through a hard link so an existing archive is never overwritten. No runtime path calls the rotation automatically.

  • Run manifests detect an edited tool list (#2017, thanks @rudycelekli). RunManifest.verify_hash covered tools_hash but not the tool names it summarises, so a run_manifest.json whose tool_names gained, lost or renamed a tool still verified. It now rebuilds the snapshot from the names and fails on a mismatch, and the evaluation harness's manifest-integrity verdict inherits the check. Untampered round trips still verify.

  • Imported amounts read dot grouping, and only valid grouping (#1964, thanks @rudycelekli). In load_cashflows and load_panel, 1.234.567 failed as an unusable amount while 1,234,567 parsed. Repeated dots now group digits too. A maintainer follow-up accepts grouping, inferred or implied by a declared decimal_separator, only when every group is valid (threes, or Indian 2-2-3 grouping), so a malformed amount or a separator inside the fraction is refused instead of concatenated. 1,234 and 12.000 still ask for decimal_separator.

Paper trading

  • Strategy steps no longer hang on the stdio MCP transport (closes #2089). The strategy worker was a multiprocessing spawn child, which on Windows could be blocked by the MCP host's pending read of the server's stdin, so every strategy_path call timed out. The worker is now a fresh interpreter that talks to the server over its own private pipes, and strategy prints never reach the protocol stream. A regression test that starts the worker while the host's stdin read is blocked now also runs in CI on native Windows x64 and ARM64. Startup and per-day deadlines, sandboxing and hard teardown are unchanged.

  • ST stocks outside the main board keep their board's price limit (#1984, thanks @rudycelekli). When the exchange limit table has no row and the band is derived from the previous close, every ST stock was given a ±5% band, so a ChiNext or STAR ST stock moving 8% had its buy or sell rejected as limit_up / limit_down. Only main-board ST stocks narrow to ±5% now; ChiNext and STAR keep ±20% and Beijing ±30%.

  • A 3:2 split priced just inside the neutral band is still caught (#1985, thanks @rudycelekli). The split detector ignored any close-to-close ratio inside (2/3, 3/2), so a 3:2 split quantized to the cent (30.00 to 20.01, a ratio of 0.667) passed as an ordinary one-third loss. A ratio within 0.1% of 2:3 or 3:2 is now a candidate: with matching volume and NAV continuity the step returns PAPER_DATA_NOT_READY, otherwise that day's orders on the symbol are rejected corporate_action_unresolved. Holdings are still never rescaled from prices alone.

Live trading, brokers and portfolio

  • Robinhood portfolio positions are priced, and long accounts are read in full (#2054, closes #1428, thanks @Jackzigen). The read-only Robinhood source now declares quotes.read and prices each equity position through get_equity_quotes instead of listing it unpriced, and position and order reads follow the next cursor to the last page; a failed follow-up page fails the read rather than returning part of the account. A quote refresh never opens a browser for OAuth. A maintainer follow-up requires the quote to name the requested symbol, prices only an active instrument that has traded, uses the newer timestamped regular- or extended-hours trade and shows its as-of time; anything else stays unpriced with a price_error. The mapping follows a third-party capture of the server's schema and has not been checked against a live account.

  • Portfolio errors no longer carry credentials, and Binance HTTP 451 is explained (#1937, thanks @goatyyc). Connector read errors go through the shared redactor before they are stored, sent as refresh progress or returned: URL query strings and signature / api_key / secret values are replaced, and the latest and historical snapshot reads clean errors stored before the upgrade. A Binance HTTP 451 "restricted location" failure gets the code binance_restricted_location and a localized explanation on the Portfolio page.

  • Longbridge keeps per-currency cash and financing limits (#1940, thanks @alexwan02). Longbridge's total_cash nets every currency into one number, so a borrowed (negative) currency leg disappeared behind a positive one. Balance rows now carry each currency's available, withdrawable, frozen and settling cash and the account's maximum and remaining financing amount, through account reads, Portfolio snapshots (balance_details), the portfolio and connector-balance CLI output, the API, the agent's portfolio context and the Web account card. They are not added to totals; negative values keep their sign and unreported ones stay empty.

  • Binance spot open orders can be listed across all symbols (closes #2090). get_open_orders without a symbol always raised, because CCXT refuses a symbol-less fetchOpenOrders by default to warn about its request weight. The spot client now acknowledges that warning; CCXT's rate limiter stays on, and exchange, authentication and network failures still surface. Fill history without a symbol still reports that Binance needs one.

  • Deleting a connection keeps its vault protection (#2153, thanks @TerryF7). Deleting an OAuth or local-gateway connection, whose profile declares no credential fields, no longer needs the OS keyring; it used to fail without the keyring extra. A connection with stored credentials is still kept when the vault is unavailable or cannot confirm the secrets were removed, and the API answers HTTP 503 with the reason instead of a server error. The connection center shows the failure on that connection's card, with a localized message when the portfolio still uses the connection.

  • Zerodha and Shoonya paper orders refuse fractional quantities (#1991, thanks @rudycelekli). The local paper simulators truncated the quantity, so 0.5 shares was acknowledged as a simulated fill of 0 shares and 2.7 as 2. A quantity must now be a finite whole number of at least 1, otherwise the order is refused with "quantity must be a positive whole number of shares". Both brokers remain paper-only.

  • The broker key . can no longer act on the global kill switch (#2003, thanks @rudycelekli). A broker's live directory is <runtime_root>/live/<broker>, and . resolved to the live root itself, so that broker's HALT sentinel was the global one: halting or clearing broker . halted or released every broker. . is now rejected alongside path separators and ...

  • MT5 keeps a configured deviation_points of 0 (#2019, thanks @rudycelekli). An explicit zero slippage tolerance was read as "unset" and replaced by 20 points on every market order; only a missing or empty value defaults to 20 now.

Shadow account

  • Shadow-account backtests trade every documented entry condition (#2073, thanks @cgycorey). The generated signal_engine.py recognised only two-sided {"min", "max"} bounds, so ("<=", -0.08) pairs, bare scalars and one-sided bounds were dropped and the backtest traded a looser rule than the profile stated. Pairs and scalars (read as >=, as the live scanner reads them) now map onto inclusive bounds, each side applies on its own, and scanner and engine share one operator vocabulary. A strict > or <, ==, != or a non-string market, which the engine cannot express exactly, now raises an error instead of being widened.

  • The shadow-account result cache no longer serves stale runs (#2075, thanks @cgycorey). A cached run was keyed by window and journal hash only, so an edited rule or market selection silently reused numbers from the old definition; the key now also covers the rules, the source and preferred markets and an engine epoch. A cached report also dropped excluded_currencies and read as if every currency had been summed; the disclosure is restored. Existing caches miss once and re-run.

Trade journal and cash flows

  • Journal buys and sells need a valid quantity (#2052, thanks @MetaAviator). A buy or sell row whose quantity was missing, zero, negative or non-finite reached FIFO matching. The analysis now returns an error asking for finite positive quantities before any filter is applied. Cash dividends may still have zero quantity.

  • Cash-flow transfers are sign-checked (#2023, thanks @dajiaohuang). transfer_in and transfer_out carried no sign rule, so a transfer written from the portfolio's side entered XIRR and money-weighted returns with the wrong sign. They now follow the holder's convention used for contributions and distributions: a transfer into the portfolio is negative and a transfer out positive, and a contradicting amount is refused with a hint to flip the sign or use another kind. The cashflow_performance schema states it.

Channels and scheduled delivery

  • Feishu tool hints land on the streaming card that is already open (#1957, thanks @Shizoqua). The hint path looked up the reply's buffer by the inbound message id, but it wrote through send_delta without that metadata, so the write resolved to the chat id instead. The hint then opened a second card whose "generating" indicator never cleared. The metadata is now forwarded, so both lookups use the same stream.

  • Teams replies to a multi-paragraph message keep the quote out of the user's text (#1958, thanks @Shizoqua). For the "Reply wrapper" shape, the parser split only at the first blank line, so later paragraphs of the quoted message reached the agent as the user's reply. It now splits at every blank line and treats only the last block as the reply. Limit: if the reply itself has several paragraphs, all but its last are folded into the quote.

  • Weixin attachments with the same name no longer overwrite each other (#1967, thanks @Shizoqua). A sender-supplied filename was written as-is into the media directory, so a second report.pdf replaced the first one, which an earlier turn still pointed to. Each download is now created exclusively under a unique name that keeps the original stem (up to 32 characters) and extension. Repeated names, identical URLs, same-second retries and concurrent downloads each get their own file. The maintainer follow-up replaced the PR's timestamp-and-hash suffix, which could still collide.

  • Voice notes reach the agent as attachments on five channels (#2078, #2106, thanks @Shizoqua and @VectorPeak). WhatsApp (#2078) and Telegram, Feishu, Matrix and Weixin (#2106) called a transcribe_audio method that no channel defines. As a result, a voice note was dropped or logged as a failed download. Each adapter now forwards the downloaded audio with the same media marker it builds for other attachments. No speech-to-text runs: the agent gets the audio file, not a transcript.

  • Discord streams no longer fail on a long first message or repeat overflow (#2114, thanks @Shizoqua). The first message of a stream sent the whole buffer, so a first delta longer than Discord's 2,000-character limit failed the stream. The first message now sends only the first chunk, as later edits already did, and the remaining text goes out once when the stream finishes. The maintainer merge removed the PR's extra sends at stream start, which would have duplicated that overflow.

  • Channel path containment has boundary tests (#2094, thanks @Shizoqua). Matrix uses is_path_within to keep outbound attachments inside the workspace when that restriction is on. It is now tested against a sibling directory that shares the root's prefix, .. traversal both out of and within the root, and a symlink that points outside. Behaviour is unchanged.

Web UI and locales

  • Stopping a chat also stops its swarm run and freezes the card (#2060, thanks @Jackzigen). The stop button cancelled only the session, so a swarm that had already started kept its card spinning, and a late event could repaint it as completed. Stop now marks every pending or running swarm card in the chat as cancelled, including its running and waiting agents. It also sends POST /swarm/runs/{id}/cancel for each run and ignores any later event for a cancelled card. Limit: the card shows cancelled even when the cancel request fails, because every error is swallowed, not only the 404 from a run that already finished.
